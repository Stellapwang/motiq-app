Pen check — per participant

Ask the participant to draw a straight line from A to B, once with each hand. This checks that the stylus registers, that samples arrive at a usable rate, and that pressure varies. It is a device check, not a trial — no trial record is written.

Sound check — per participant

Play both tones at the level the session will use. The participant must hear the difference easily — this is not a discrimination test. Fix the volume here and do not change it during the session.
