Screen calibration

Hold a bank card flat on the screen over the outline. Any card works — they are all 85.60 × 53.98 mm. Adjust until the outline matches it exactly. Without this the spiral is the wrong physical size and nothing else is meaningful.

Saved trials on this device
Trials are written here the moment they end, so a failed download or a reload costs nothing. This is browser storage, not a database — clearing Safari's website data erases it. Export at the end of every session.

CSV is one flat table: session details repeat on every row and row_type marks each row as a pen sample, a tap, or a tone. JSON is the complete nested record. t_ms counts from the moment START was pressed; pen samples, taps and tone onsets share that origin.
